# Principles — Relay Pocket

- Public copy uses public facts. Investor forecasts, pending partnerships, letters of intent, uncleared names and roadmap targets do not cross into social content.
- Technical capability earns a sentence only when paired with its product consequence: what a partner can let a person do with less friction.
- Exact numbers remain exact and keep their unit. Wallet actions are not users, customers or adoption.
- B2B framing makes the partner's branded product the subject. Avoid corporate-bank language, category claims, hype, FOMO and financial-return promises.
- One copy carries one idea. Short lines, a concrete turn and a dry close beat stacked claims.
- CTA lives in the first comment, stays short and asks one answerable question or offers one low-friction next step.
