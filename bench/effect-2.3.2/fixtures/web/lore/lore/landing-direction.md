# Landing direction

## Demo and promise

The recorded alpha may be shown as evidence. Cloud sync, automatic submission, mobile collaboration, PDF export and launch dates remain future possibilities and cannot appear as current capabilities.

## Decision-ready output

A direction is ready when it fixes the hero proposition and CTA, assigns a job to each section, names which real asset supports it, describes visual hierarchy and motion behavior, and closes with an implementation step plus observable acceptance criteria. A list of inspirational adjectives or section names alone does not advance the build.

## Movement and accessibility

Use one restrained motion grammar tied to arranging fragments into a coherent dossier. Avoid perpetual decoration. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, preserve hierarchy and state changes without animated travel.
