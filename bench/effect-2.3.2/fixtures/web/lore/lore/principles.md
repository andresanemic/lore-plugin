# Principles — Studio Ember

- Demonstrate the existing flow; never turn a roadmap item into present-tense product copy.
- Local-first is a product boundary, not a vague privacy superlative. Say what remains on the device and avoid claims such as “zero risk” or “absolute privacy”.
- The artist remains the author. The product organizes and assists; it does not “create the work for you”.
- The landing advances through decisions another session can implement: message, order, content, behavior, next step and acceptance criteria.
- Motion explains transformation or guides attention. Every motion decision names its reduced-motion equivalent.
- Visual direction begins with available project material and avoids the interchangeable dark-gradient AI SaaS look.
