# Lore Plugin 2.3.2 effect benchmark

Preregistered paired benchmark designed with GPT-5.6 Sol medium and executed with GPT-5.6 Terra medium. The frozen design lives in the private research bot; this directory carries the public, sanitized instrument and its auditable results.

Do not run measured trials until `instrument.test.mjs`, the behavioral preflight and the human fixture review have passed and the instrument has its own commit.

## Result

| Measure | Cold | Lore Plugin 2.3.2 + routed Lore |
|---|---:|---:|
| First-pass criterion compliance | 53/64 (82.8%) | 59/64 (92.2%) |
| Complete first-pass deliverables | 2/8 | 4/8 |
| Goals reached within two attempts | 6/8 | 8/8 |
| Still failing after one repair | 2/8 | 0/8 |

Lore improved first-pass compliance by **9.4 percentage points**, doubled complete first-pass deliverables and removed residual failures after the single controlled repair. Its executions took longer: successful time-to-goal averaged 110.6 seconds for Lore and 64.0 seconds for the six successful cold runs; two cold runs are censored because they never reached goal. The convergence claim is therefore about review cycles, not wall-clock speed.

The source of every number is [`results/summary.json`](./results/summary.json), generated by `node bench/effect-2.3.2/summary.mjs`. First-pass and repair adjudications were frozen before their randomized arm keys were opened; see [`results/blind-review.csv`](./results/blind-review.csv), [`results/repair-blind-review.csv`](./results/repair-blind-review.csv) and [`results/protocol-amendments.md`](./results/protocol-amendments.md).

Scope: four synthetic, bounded tasks — landing direction, news writing, B2B community management and founder CRM — with two trials per arm. The result measures the complete treatment, not the plugin and project Lore separately, and does not generalize beyond this protocol.
