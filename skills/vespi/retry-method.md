# Retry stop: coordinator extension

The coordinator enforces the stop in dispatch and durable execution, and preserves it across FASES reloads. Use a real host lookup before recording `operation retry-search` with observedBy, tool, query, findings (source, finding, limit, applies), the named change and same_scope. This is coordinator attestation, not authenticated search evidence. Without a search tool, record available:false and reason; the operation remains blocked. A receipt covers only its recorded failure stretch; a later repeated failure blocks again. A recorded conflict remains pending owner arbitration and cannot be erased by a subsequent clean lookup.

Dispatch the stopped task with retryChange matching the lookup. The returned task's retry_execution carries that change and search_digest; include it in the worker's instructions and require Advisor/verifier to contrast it with the delivery. This field attests the coordinator's instruction, not real application by the worker. A direct durable capability call cannot substitute for that dispatch. Closure remains blocked until the search or arbitration is resolved; cancellation/abandonment is a separate owner decision. Neither observations nor lookup receipts may claim future times.

This extension belongs to the plugin coordinator. The pinned kernel method remains unchanged.
