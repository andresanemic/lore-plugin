# Cards in an operation

Offer via `operation card offer` with decisionBefore and decisionPoint (dimension; two viable options with distinct id/consequence), or a three-failure wall with a recorded lookup. Never use an isolated word as a trigger. One unsolicited offer per operation; preserve its receipt before showing the prompt. No routine offers in mechanical/repetitive work. The user may request another; record that request.

`operation card answer` records accepted/declined/ignored. Do not infer a response from silence. None of these determines class. `operation card arbitrate` takes posterior evidence linked to cardId, source, by, at, decisionBefore, decisionAfter, description and kind. Opening plus distinction and changed decision yields germen; friction plus reverted/discarded disposition and reversalReason yields ruido (reverted returns to prior decision); evaluation plus unchanged decision yields neutral. Missing/contradictory evidence stays pending. Preserve classification history and source of attestation. Full rule: `docs/arbitraje-baraja.md`; approved 2026-10-08. Coordinator/verifier assess the described evidence; these fields cannot authenticate causal effect or human experience.

The receipt is not a phenomenological declaration. Cards never grant authority, change scope, clear retry stops or replace domain verification.

The async prompt helper requires root and operation ID, reads the persisted offer, and records prompt_prepared_at before returning. It prepares once in the agreed sequential coordinator; it does not attest actual delivery to a host or concurrent process exclusion. Without a persisted offer it returns null.
