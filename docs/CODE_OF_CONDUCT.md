# Lore Plugin Code of Conduct

Lore is built through disagreement, correction, and evidence. Those only work when people can
participate without being attacked or pushed aside.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas, claims, code, and evidence — never the person presenting them.
- Make room for different backgrounds, identities, languages, experience levels, and ways of working.
- Assume good faith, while taking responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.
- Respect privacy and stop when someone asks you to stop.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, or publishing private material without permission are not welcome. Repeatedly
disrupting a discussion after a maintainer asks you to stop is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public space represented
as part of the Lore Plugin community. Conduct outside those spaces may be considered when it creates
a credible safety risk for participants here.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to [andres@healthproof.cl](mailto:andres@healthproof.cl). Include links,
screenshots, or other context when possible. Reports will be handled as confidentially as the
situation allows.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, limit participation, or permanently
exclude someone when needed to protect the community. Anyone handling a report in which they have a
conflict of interest must step aside.

Questions about this code can be raised in the
[repository discussions](https://github.com/andresanemic/lore-plugin/discussions) or sent privately
to the address above.
