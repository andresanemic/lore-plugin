# Contributing

> [← Back to the README](../README.md) · [Versión en español](./CONTRIBUTING_es.md) · [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

Contributions are welcome when their boundary is explicit. Lore Plugin separates product behavior, product evidence and LUS research so that a useful observation does not silently become a universal claim.

## Choose the right path

- **Bugs, documentation and product changes:** open an [issue](https://github.com/andresanemic/lore-plugin/issues) or a pull request.
- **Cases and refutations:** use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/andresanemic/lore-plugin/discussions) and state the context, what happened, the claimed boundary and what would disprove the reading.
- **Ideas and questions:** begin in Discussions when no concrete patch exists yet.
- **LUS research:** identify the relevant hypothesis and distinguish observation, interpretation and evidence. A Discussion or product case is not admitted to the scientific corpus automatically.

Do not include private Lore, credentials, passphrases or third-party material you cannot publish.

## Pull requests

1. Keep the change as small as the claim permits.
2. Update English and Spanish documents together when the same public statement appears in both.
3. Preserve declared evidence boundaries; do not upgrade cases into proofs or hypotheses into product promises.
4. Run `npm test` and check every relative Markdown link you changed.
5. Describe what changed, why, and what was intentionally left outside scope.

Documentation-only changes ordinarily need no version bump. Tags and GitHub Releases are created only when maintainers deliberately cut a product release; historical release records are not rewritten to absorb later documentation edits.

By participating, you agree to the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
