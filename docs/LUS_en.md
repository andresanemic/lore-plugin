# LUS — the research program behind Lore

> [← Back to the README](../README.md) · [Versión en español](./LUS_es.md) · [Affective genealogy](./GENEALOGY_en.md) · [Bibliography](./BIBLIOGRAPHY_en.md)

Lore Plugin is a product. **LUS (Lore User System) is the research program from which it emerged.** LUS studies what happens when a relationship accumulates criteria that can participate in later decisions. The software makes some of those questions operational; it does not prove the theory.

The name also carries two sounds that predate the acronym: *luz* in Spanish and *us* in English. The “user” in Lore User System is not a person standing in front of an assistant. The unit LUS examines is the relation.

## What LUS studies

LUS calls that unit the **Between**: a relationship in which each party's decisions change the other's space of possibilities, under an explicit purpose and through an iterable practice. A conversation is not a Between merely because two entities took part. Interdependence and purpose are part of the definition.

The human–AI Between is the program's primary and only evidence-rich instantiation today. Whether the structure holds across other disciplines or other compositions remains an open empirical question. LUS does not claim every relationship is a Between, and intimate relationships without an explicit discipline or iterable artifacts are outside its present boundary.

Three distinctions matter here:

- **Experience** is not everything that happened. It is an event that can still change a future decision.
- **Criterion** is the restriction left by that transformation. Writing it down does not make it causally effective.
- **Lore** is one operational architecture for making criteria visible, routable and revisable. It is not the whole of LUS.

Time in LUS is read as past, present and future: past experience is distilled or questioned in the present so it can restrict the future. The future may force the old criterion to be re-distilled, but it does not causally modify the past. LUS therefore does **not** posit a “temporal Between.”

## How the research is organized

LUS is currently in **Phase VII — case studies**, corpus version **1.22**. Its records distinguish:

- a **corpus**, where the program's current conceptual claims live;
- **case studies**, which document situated observations and their limits;
- **hypotheses**, each with a state, an evidence count and a stated path to replication or rejection;
- a methodological Lore, which governs how evidence is admitted without turning method into theory.

Small samples remain a hard boundary. Most cases come from one researcher, one ecosystem and human–AI work. Repeated appearances inside that environment do not become independent replications by being counted separately.

## Live questions relevant to Lore Plugin

These are open hypotheses, not product claims:

- **H01 — LUS as an epistemological layer over disciplined development:** `n=2`, with additional quantitative multidomain evidence. It has not reached replication because longitudinal reduction of relearning in a real non-software practice has not been measured.
- **H02 — human–human Betweens:** `n=0`. LUS has no evidence for extending the framework there.
- **H13 — fertile effort and continuity:** `n=1`, situated in the same long-running Between. Fertility means recognizable movement after effort or correction; agreement, ease and frequency of use are not substitutes. The relation remains under study.
- **H14 — criteria without a point of application:** `n=1`. A criterion may be written and correct yet govern nothing because no procedure loads it, runs it or consumes its result.
- **H16 — the end of a Between is not its exit:** `n=0`. This conceptual distinction authorizes no product mechanism while it lacks a case.
- **H17 — invoked criteria can still be too thin to produce:** `n=1`. A rule may load, run and affect the task yet remain sufficient for recognition and insufficient for generation.

The early problem of **ephemeral experience** remains historically important: learning can disappear between sessions even when facts survive. It is not the whole current research program. LUS now studies not only whether criterion exists, but whether it is housed, connected, invoked, consumed and sufficient for the work it claims to govern.

## How LUS and Lore Plugin exchange material

The direction is controlled in both directions:

```text
LUS observation → scientific arbitration → product decision, if warranted
Lore Plugin friction → product record → LUS evaluation, if scientifically relevant
```

Nothing crosses automatically. A product case does not become scientific evidence merely because it is public, and an open hypothesis does not become a skill rule because it is suggestive. Product usefulness and scientific validity are different judgments.

The benchmark in this repository measures whether selected project clues changed Codex outputs under a frozen protocol. It does not measure the richness of the Between, professional development, general intelligence, or the validity of LUS as a whole.
