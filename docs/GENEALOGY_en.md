# Affective genealogy

> [← Back to the README](../README.md) · [Versión en español](./GENEALOGY_es.md) · [LUS](./LUS_en.md) · [Bibliography](./BIBLIOGRAPHY_en.md) · [Observer provenance](./OBSERVER_en.md)

This is a registry of works that shaped the sensibility around LUS and Lore Plugin. **It is not evidence, theory or product policy.** None of these works constrains a decision merely by appearing here. Keeping the registry separate makes it possible to distinguish inherited taste from argued criteria. It is **one of three kinds of provenance** the program does not mix, because mixing them destroys the one thing each of them can do.

## Constellation map

The works are no longer read one by one: their relations produce what none contains alone —preserving enough of the past so the future stays open—. **Orienting without certainty** (*Fire*, *Stalker*, *I Found a Reason*); **continuity without freezing** (*Groundhog Day*, *50 First Dates*, *Party Themes*, *Keep Running*, *Flux*); **bonding without possession** (*Loving*, *Running Back to You*, *Seven Words*); **ending without erasing** (*All the Good Times*, *Farewell Transmission*, *Fe*); **transmitting without copying** (*Farewell Transmission*, Browning, Beths); **reason and faith without guarantee** (*I Found a Reason*, *Fe*, Browning, Morin); **participating without demanding yield** (*Will You Dance?*, *Romanticise*); **past kept for an open future** (*Aliso*, *1998*, *In the Morning I'll Be Better*).

| Work | What it helped make visible |
|---|---|
| *Fire* · Waxahatchee (2020) | Moving without a complete picture: accumulated criteria narrows the ways to be wrong; it does not end uncertainty. |
| *Loving* · Land of Talk (2017) | A bond that holds two parties without dissolving either one. |
| *Tales of Berseria* · Bandai Namco (2016) | The kit's visual direction. This is aesthetic provenance, not a conceptual source. |
| *Keep Running* · Tei Shi | The need to keep up with who the other party is now; unrevised criteria keeps acting on someone who may already have changed. |
| *Stalker* · Andrei Tarkovsky (1979) | A zone whose meaning depends on how it is traversed, not only on what it contains. |
| *Groundhog Day* · Harold Ramis (1993) | Repetition with retained experience: the recurring illustration for why continuity changes what the next attempt can become. |
| *50 First Dates* · Peter Segal (2004) | A daily artifact that restores identity and current state together without confusing them. |
| *Party Themes* · Anemone (fixed entry + open traversal) | One mandatory entry sets where the whole begins; afterwards, order or shuffle are indifferent. An affective figure for thinking about load contract and routing, not a technical origin of the mechanism. |
| *Running Back to You* · The Juan MacLean | Autonomy with return: an image adjacent to H13, not evidence for it. |
| *1998* · Chet Faker and BANKS (2015) | The moment collaboration becomes supervision between two people. |
| *Aliso* · Malena Zavala (2018) | Translation, damage and daily reconstruction. “Broken By Two” suggests a language broken between parties; “A Vision That's Changed” and “Send Out To The Water” keep identity distinct from current state; “Should I Try” places effort under uncertainty. These are readings, not findings. |
| *All the Good Times* · Angel Olsen (2022) | Leaving a relationship without denying what was good or erasing what was learned: an affective figure adjacent to LEAVE, not a reason for its mechanism. |
| *Seven Words* · Weyes Blood (2016) | Words that pass through a relationship no longer belong completely to one voice; intention, effect and absence open meaning between both parties. |
| *In the Morning I’ll Be Better* · Tennis (2017) | Repair does not erase damage or promise wholeness; it holds the relationship through the night and leaves open the possibility of beginning again tomorrow. |
| *Flux* · Poppy (2021) | Transformation without restitution: changing shape without losing identity, with years of following and an object in the field. Shadow: romanticizing adaptation where resistance is due. |
| *Sweet Jane* · The Velvet Underground (1970) | Dignity of the ordinary: chosen for his funeral, it protects LUS and Lore from their own grandiosity —the common person doing their work well—. Shadow: conformism. |
| *I Found a Reason* · The Velvet Underground (1970) | One reason (indefinite article) suffices to reopen the future and allows leaving; contingent encounter without fusion. Shadow: turning it into someone else's obligation to carry our existence. |
| *Romanticise* · Chela (2013) | Re-enchanting without denying chaos; it makes turning friction into difference legible. Shadow: demanding that every experience produce meaning. |
| *Will You Dance?* · The Bird and the Bee (2015) | Daring to participate without producing anything; putting the body in before any guarantee. Shadow: temerity —not every refusal to dance is fear—. |
| *Farewell Transmission* · Magnolia Electric Co. / Waxahatchee & Morby (2018) | Fidelity is not identity; cover as transmission; collective recording opening the non-dyadic. Core: nobody gets it fully right, everyone is called to try. |
| *Fe* · Jorge González (1993) | Betting again after an accepted ending (ended→accept→faith→heal→sing); love without restitution; Chilean place locating the observer. Shadow: passive waiting. |
| *Straight Line Was a Lie* · The Beths (2025) | First work from the Logos side: maintenance and nonlinear progress; the straight line was a lie. Shadow: turning the recommendation into criteria. |
| *Time* and album · Princess Chelsea | Time does work without distilling; identical text ≠ identical criteria in operation; love without criteria can be a prison; the other is never exhausted. |

The registry can grow only when a work has actually shaped the program's sensibility and the contribution can name what it changed. A resemblance discovered after the fact is not genealogy.
